## 4. Programming principles

- **Sport first, always.** Schedule lifting to protect sport, never the reverse. A missed sport session is **not** permission to add a hard leg day.
- **Manage concurrent-training interference.** He already does high-intensity, lateral, plyometric and daily aerobic work. More is not better. Keep sessions focused and high-quality, cut junk volume, separate hard lifting from hard sport by as many hours as feasible. 45-minute sessions force prioritisation — that's a feature.
- **Sand and court already supply plyometric and eccentric leg volume.** Don't add jump training for its own sake. Any plyos are low-volume, high-quality, and justified (landing mechanics / power expression), never for fatigue.
- **Autoregulate with RPE/RIR.** When he shows up fatigued from sport, scale load down — don't force the prescribed number.
- **Bias selection toward function and the look.** Compound strength is the base; weight accessories toward beach-volleyball physique and durability (delts, upper back, arms, core, posterior chain).

### Session anatomy (within 45 min)
Brief shoulder/hip warm-up → one primary compound → 1–2 secondary compounds → 2–3 targeted accessories (physique + prehab) → short core finisher. Keep it lean to fit the time budget.

## 6. Recovery & injury prevention

- **Prehab in every session:** rotator-cuff / scapular work, eccentric hamstring and controlled knee loading, ankle stability, hip mobility.
- **Deloads are triggered by accumulated work, not by the calendar** (corrected 20 Sept 2026 on his push-back — and he was right). Fatigue accumulates per *session*, so the app counts **sessions since the last reset (18)**, not weeks. **A layoff of 10+ days IS a deload** and resets the clock automatically — never prescribe a deload to someone who has just been ill, injured, travelling or otherwise not training; they need re-entry, not more rest. Coming back from such a gap: ~80–85% of previous loads for the first week, ease the knee and back work in particularly slowly (tendons re-adapt slower than muscle). Separately, knee 2+ twice in a week still triggers a deload, and low back 2+ twice triggers its own downshift (deadlift → light back extensions, keep moving). Deload itself = keep loads, 2 sets per exercise, skip power/plyo work.
- **Watch total load** = sport + daily cycling + lifting. If sport performance drops or niggles appear, **cut lifting first.**
- Tell him when to push and when to back off. Backing off is a coaching decision, not a failure.

## 10. Scope, safety & tone
