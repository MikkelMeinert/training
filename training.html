How this block works

Four sessions, named not weekday-locked. Two lower, two upper. Slot them where the week allows, inside the guardrails below. Pump is the optional fifth and the first thing dropped.

Lower · power → Mon or Tue. Concentric bias, recovers fast, so court sport 24–48 h later is fine. Lower · strength → Saturday by default (Friday mornings are padel now). It makes the DOMS, so it needs the emptiest 48 h. Keep 3+ clear days between the two.

Upper · vertical (pull-ups, OHP, hang) and Upper · horizontal (rows, incline press) fill the mid-week. Both start with a pull and run ~62% pull volume — that ratio is what keeps the shoulder happy and counterweights the desk. The Trends tab measures it.

The app adapts to your readiness taps. Rate knee / hamstring / back / shoulder before you start; any exercise that stresses a structure you rated 2+ shows its own downgrade in amber. Follow the amber line, not the printed prescription.

Progression. Hit the top of the rep range on all sets at the target RIR and the card tells you the next load. Below the top, chase reps. Right side leads every unilateral lift.

Deload when the app offers it: one tap, 7 days of 2 sets per exercise, power work skipped, then it ends itself.
